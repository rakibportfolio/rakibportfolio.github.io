Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we begin your project.

How fast can we receive the first video draft?
For most social reels, shorts, and SaaS promos, our initial cut turnaround is between 48 to 72 hours. Larger, multi-scene 3D animations or full commercial campaigns typically take 5-7 business days.
How does the revision and feedback process work?
We use Frame.io, Google Drive, and Notion for seamless collaboration. You can pause at any exact second and leave timestamps or notes. We refine until you are 100% happy with every single frame.
Do you work with startups on ongoing monthly retainers?
Yes! Over 70% of our clients work with us on a dedicated monthly retainer (e.g. 12-20 vertical videos/month, or continuous product launches). Retainers give you priority turnaround and a dedicated editor.
Can we schedule a quick discovery call first?
Absolutely! Reach out via WhatsApp or submit the form above, and we can immediately jump on a quick 15-minute Google Meet or Zoom discovery call to align on your vision and scope.

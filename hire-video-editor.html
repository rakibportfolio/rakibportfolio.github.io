✦ TERMS & CONDITIONS

Before You Apply

Please read these terms carefully. By applying, you agree to follow these working rules. These exist to protect both you and us.

📜

Working Terms

Effective for all freelance editors working with Zentic Media

01

💻  Project-Based Work Only

All work at Zentic Media is project-based. You will be assigned individual video projects with clear briefs, timelines, and deliverables. There is no fixed monthly salary — payment is made per completed project.

02

💵  Advance Payment is Required to Start Work

Before any project begins, the client must pay an advance to Zentic Media. Work on a project will not start until the advance has been received and confirmed. If a client does not pay the advance, the project will not proceed — and no work should be started by the editor under any circumstances.

03

🚫  No Advance = No Work

This is a strict rule. Do not begin any editing work until you receive confirmation from us that the client's advance payment has been received. Starting work without advance confirmation will not be compensated.

04

🔔  Stay Active & Responsive

You must remain as active as possible during working hours. When we send you a message, we expect a reply within a reasonable time. Slow or no responses delay projects and affect client relationships. If you're unavailable, inform us in advance.

05

🕑  Deadlines Are Non-Negotiable

You must meet every deadline 100% of the time. Late deliveries damage our reputation with clients and will not be tolerated. If you foresee a delay, you must communicate it to us before the deadline — not after. Consistent late delivery will result in termination.

06

🔒  Client Confidentiality

All client information, project files, briefs, and footage shared with you are strictly confidential. You may not share, publish, or use any client material without explicit written permission from Zentic Media and the client.

07

🌟  Quality Standards

Every deliverable must meet our quality standards before submission. Rushed, low-effort work will be sent back for revision. We value quality over speed — but both matter. Take pride in your work.

08

🔁  Revisions

You must be willing to make reasonable revisions based on client or team feedback. Revisions that fall within the original project scope are expected as part of the project and will not be separately charged to the client.

09

📈  Performance & Growth

Editors who consistently deliver quality work on time, communicate professionally, and show initiative will be considered for higher rates, more projects, and long-term retainer contracts. We reward dedication.

10

🤐  Mutual Respect

We run a professional and respectful environment. We expect editors to treat our team and clients with respect. In return, we commit to being fair, transparent, and supportive in every interaction.
